# Watching and listening to Alabama games

## Where games are on TV

- Since 2024, SEC games air on the ESPN/ABC family: ABC, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, SEC Network, and SEC Network+ / ESPN+ (streaming only). The SEC's old CBS package moved to ABC/ESPN that year.
- Non-conference games can land elsewhere; for example, Alabama's 2024 game at Wisconsin aired on FOX.
- The SEC usually announces networks and kickoff times 6 to 12 days ahead, so a game far in the future may show "TBD".
- The schedule in Bama Hub always shows the current network for each game.

## Cheapest legal ways to watch

- ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. A TV antenna (a one-time purchase, no subscription) picks them up free from local stations. That covers every Alabama game on ABC. The FCC's reception maps (fcc.gov/media/engineering/dtvmaps) show which stations reach an address.
- ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU and SEC Network need a pay-TV or live-TV streaming subscription, or an ESPN subscription, and stream in the ESPN app with that login.
- SEC Network+ and ESPN+ games are streaming-only in the ESPN app with an ESPN+ or ESPN subscription.
- Live-TV streaming services (YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo, Sling, DIRECTV) carry different channel lineups by plan and market. Check a plan includes ESPN, SEC Network and your local ABC before a big game.
- Prices and packages change often; check the provider's site for current details.
- There is no official one-time "season pass" that streams every Alabama game, and ABC/ESPN don't offer an API for other apps to stream their games. Bama Hub links you to the official apps instead.

## Radio: the Crimson Tide Sports Network

- Every game is called live on the Crimson Tide Sports Network, Alabama's official radio network, carried by affiliate stations around the state.
- Eli Gold was the play-by-play "Voice of the Crimson Tide" for more than three decades starting in 1988. Chris Stewart took over play-by-play in the 2020s. Check RollTide.com for the current broadcast crew.
- Ways to listen live:
  - A local affiliate station on AM/FM radio (free).
  - RollTide.com, the official athletics site, and the Varsity Network app, which carries many schools' official radio broadcasts.
  - SiriusXM, which carries SEC games on its college sports channels and in the SXM app.
- Some online radio streams of games are restricted to listeners near the school or require the official app, because of broadcast rights.

## Syncing the radio call with the TV picture

- Many fans watch on TV with the radio call playing. Broadcast and streaming TV usually run several seconds to a minute behind the live radio feed.
- Bama Hub's Radio page can delay its stream by up to about three minutes. Start the radio, then nudge the delay until the call matches the picture.
- Streaming TV (YouTube TV, Hulu, the ESPN app) usually needs a longer delay than an antenna or cable.
